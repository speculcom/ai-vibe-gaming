AI 做游戏Vibe Gaming

学习路径Learning path

按做游戏的实际顺序分五阶段，不按工具组织。每阶段四件事：做什么 · 怎么做 · 常见失败 · 我们的判断。Five stages in the order you actually build a game, not organised by tool. Each covers four things: what, how, common failures, and our judgement.

五个阶段The five stages

1 S1 · 选引擎S1 · Pick an engine 选错引擎，后面每一阶段都在替它的短板还债。Pick the wrong engine and every later stage ends up paying off its weaknesses. 1 Web 导出可行性Web export feasibility 「能导出到网页」和「导出后能正常用」是两件事 —— 差别通常在存档与性能。"Can export to the web" and "works after export" are different claims — the gap usually shows up in saves and performance. 2 S2 · 核心循环S2 · The core loop 玩法原型是可验证的 —— 代码能跑，就说明逻辑对。A gameplay prototype is verifiable — if the code runs, the logic is right. 2 提示词的结构Structuring the prompt 同样是「做一个跳跃游戏」，给不给边界决定了 AI 写出的是脚手架还是能跑的东西。For the same request — "make a platformer" — whether you give boundaries decides whether AI writes scaffolding or something that runs. 2 最小可玩原型Minimal playable prototype 「能操作、有目标、有反馈」—— 三条都满足就可以称为原型，其余都可以先没有。"Operable, goal-directed, responsive" — hit all three and it is a prototype; everything else can wait. 2 关卡数据的组织方式How to organise level data 关卡写死在代码里，每次改关卡都要 AI 重写一大段代码 —— 这是 AI 迭代最容易失控的地方。Levels hardcoded in the code mean every level change asks AI to rewrite a big chunk of it — the easiest place for AI iteration to spiral out of control. 3 S3 · 手感调参S3 · Game feel & tuning 这是 AI 帮不上忙的一段 —— 也是整个流程里唯一必须你自己判断的。This is the stage AI cannot help with — and the only one in the whole process that requires your own judgement. 3 手感问题清单Game feel checklist 「手感不对」是六种不同的病，每种对应不同的参数。先诊断再改。"It does not feel right" is six different problems, each with its own parameter. Diagnose first, then tune. 4 S4 · 资产合规S4 · Asset licensing 这一步不是技术问题，是法律问题 —— 但它能让你的整个项目作废。This step is not technical, it is legal — and it can void your entire project. 4 字体的授权陷阱Font licensing traps 每个人都会检查图片和音频的授权，很少有人检查字体 —— 而绝大多数免费字体不含商用授权。Everyone checks the licence on images and audio; almost nobody checks fonts — and most free fonts do not cover commercial use. 4 音频素材的来源与授权Audio sources and their licences 图片你会挑、字体你会查，音频却常常随手抓一个就用 —— 而它是发布后最容易被下架的一类。You pick images carefully and check fonts, but audio usually gets grabbed at random — and it is the category most likely to get your game taken down after release. 5 S5 · 导出与发布S5 · Ship it 这一步 AI 帮得最少 —— 因为它是确定性工程，不是生成任务。This is where AI helps least — because it is deterministic engineering, not a generation task. 5 先发极简版Ship a rough version first 游戏行业的最大风险不是「做得不好玩」，而是「你不知道它好不好玩」。The biggest risk in this industry is not "the game is bad" — it is "you do not know whether it is bad". 5 存档与离线行为Save and offline behaviour 这两个是最容易在上线后才发现的问题，而且都会直接让玩家流失。These are the two things you discover after launch, and both of them lose players directly.

关键词Keywords

不属于单一阶段的概念。Concepts that do not belong to a single stage.

  • Vibe GamingVibe Gaming 跟 AI 聊天把游戏做出来。但这句话要先拆开，否则会误判。Making a game by talking to AI. But that sentence has to be taken apart first, or you will misjudge it.
  • AI 资产台账AI asset ledger 一份记录「每个资产从哪来、能不能商用」的表格。它不是法务要求，是保命要求。A table recording where each asset came from and whether it can be used commercially. It is not a legal formality — it is what saves the project.
  • Godot 的场景树Godot's scene tree Godot 用 .tscn 文本文件存场景 —— 但它不该由人手写，也不该由 AI 写。Godot stores scenes in .tscn text files — but they should be written by neither humans nor AI.
  • AI 生成内容的版权归属Copyright of AI-generated work 「AI 生成的就都是我的」—— 这不一定成立，且各司法辖区结论不同。"AI made it, so it's mine" — not necessarily true, and jurisdictions disagree.
  • 上下文窗口与 AI 写游戏Context window and AI-written games 游戏代码天然是大文件、多文件、强耦合 —— 这正好是上下文窗口最弱的场景。Game code is naturally large, multi-file and tightly coupled — exactly where the context window is weakest.

引擎档案Engine profiles

6 个引擎对 AI 的友好度。⚠ 这是本站的判断，不是官方评级 —— 依据写在每个档案里。6 engines by how AI-friendly they are. ⚠ This is the site's judgement, not an official rating — the reasoning is in each profile.

GodotGodot友好friendly
对 AI 最不容易出错的通用引擎 —— 原因不在引擎本身，在 GDScript 这门语言。The general engine least likely to trip AI up — and the reason is not the engine, it is the GDScript language.
PhaserPhaser友好friendly
2D 网页框架里最完整的一个 —— 物理、场景、资源加载全都内置；TypeScript/JavaScript 又是 AI 最擅长的语言。The most complete 2D web framework — physics, scenes and asset loading are all built in, and TypeScript/JavaScript is what AI writes best.
PixiPixi友好friendly
2D 网页游戏里对 AI 最友好的选择 —— 因为 TypeScript 是 AI 最擅长的语言之一。The most AI-friendly choice for 2D web games — because TypeScript is one of the languages AI is best at.
Three.jsThree.js友好friendly
对 AI 最友好的 3D 方案 —— 但它不是引擎，缺的东西要自己补。The most AI-friendly 3D option — but it is not an engine, and the gaps are yours to fill.
libGDXlibGDX一般mixed
Java 生态里唯一的完整游戏引擎 —— AI 写得不错，但你得先接受 JVM 的构建流程。The only complete game engine in the Java ecosystem — AI handles it decently, but you have to accept the JVM build process first.
BevyBevy不友好unfriendly
AI 最容易写错的主流引擎 —— 但它的架构可能是最干净的。The mainstream engine AI gets wrong most easily — and yet its architecture may be the cleanest.
