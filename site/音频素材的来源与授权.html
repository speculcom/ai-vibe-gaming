Audio sources and their licences

这一步决定什么What this stage decides：图片你会挑、字体你会查，音频却常常随手抓一个就用 —— 而它是发布后最容易被下架的一类。: You pick images carefully and check fonts, but audio usually gets grabbed at random — and it is the category most likely to get your game taken down after release.

本站的判断Our judgement：优先用明确标 CC0 的素材包；用 CC-BY 就把署名写进游戏；生成音乐先读服务条款再进台账。: Prefer packs explicitly marked CC0; if you use CC-BY, put the attribution in the game; for generated music, read the service terms before it enters the ledger.

性质：部分可核验Nature: partly verifiable

一句话：音频的风险不在「有没有钱」，在授权类型 —— CC0、CC-BY、NC（禁商用）长得都很像，后果完全不同。

In one line: the risk in audio is not money, it is the licence type — CC0, CC-BY and NC (no commercial use) look alike and mean very different things.

三种授权，先学会认

标记能商用吗要署名吗典型来源
CC0（公有领域）✅不需要Kenney 全部素材、Freesound 部分条目
CC-BY 4.0✅⚠ 必须署名OpenGameArt 大量条目
⚠ 含 NC 条款（NonCommercial）❌ 不能商用——Freesound 大量条目

⚠ 最容易犯的错：在素材站看到「免费」就用，没看到旁边小小的 NC 标记。

Freesound 的官方 FAQ 明确写着：条目各有不同许可，含 NonCommercial 条款的不能商用（出处见下表）。

Three licences — learn to tell them apart

MarkCommercial OKAttributionTypical source
CC0 (public domain)YesNot requiredAll of Kenney's assets, some Freesound entries
CC-BY 4.0Yes⚠ Attribution requiredMany OpenGameArt entries
⚠ NC clause (NonCommercial)❌ Not commercial—Many Freesound entries

⚠ The classic mistake: seeing "free" on an asset site and using it, missing the small NC mark next to it.

Freesound's own FAQ states plainly that entries carry different licences and those with a NonCommercial clause cannot be used commercially (source in the table below).

署名不是可选项

用了 CC-BY 素材，署名要真的进游戏：

  • 网页游戏：credits 页或 itch.io 页面描述里写明素材名、作者、许可链接
  • 商店发布：设置页或片尾滚动里放同样内容

⚠ 只写在开发文档里不算 —— 玩家看不到就等于没署（这是 CC-BY 的常见违约方式）。

Attribution is not optional

If you use CC-BY material, the credit has to actually ship in the game:

  • Web games: name the asset, author and licence link on a credits page or the itch.io page description
  • Store releases: the same content in a settings screen or end credits

⚠ Writing it only in dev docs does not count — if players cannot see it, it is a breach (the most common CC-BY violation).

生成音乐：先读条款再进台账

生成式音乐工具（Suno、Udio 一类）的商用权取决于你当时的订阅档位与服务条款，

而且条款会变 —— 见 AI 生成内容的版权归属。

⚠ 台账里生成音乐一律先写「⚠ 待确认商用条款」，发布前必须清零（见 AI 资产台账）。

Generated music: read the terms before the ledger

Commercial rights for generative music tools (Suno, Udio and the like) depend on your subscription tier and terms at the time, and terms change — see Copyright of AI-generated work.

⚠ In the ledger, generated music always starts as "⚠ commercial terms unconfirmed", and that row must be cleared before shipping (see the AI asset ledger).
